Privacy & Cookies

Last updated: 3 September 2026

GCLI uses this website to share information about global collaborative learning and to receive membership and contact enquiries. This page explains the public-site choices available to visitors. For questions or privacy requests, please use the GCLI contact form.

Essential storage

We use browser storage only to remember your cookie choice. This is necessary to respect that choice and does not enable analytics.

Optional analytics

With your permission, GCLI uses Google Analytics to understand aggregated use of the public site, such as page views and interaction patterns. Analytics is not loaded until you select Accept analytics. You can refuse it without affecting access to the public site, or change your choice at any time using Cookie settings.

Google acts as a service provider for Analytics. Its processing may involve transfers outside the European Economic Area; see Google's Privacy Policy for its current information.

External services

Public pages use Google Fonts for typography, and the membership application is provided through Google Forms. These services may receive technical connection data when you choose to use them. The membership form collects the information shown in that form; it is used only for the stated application and follow-up purposes.

Forms and accounts

When you submit the GCLI membership application or contact form, GCLI uses the information you provide to respond to your enquiry, review membership applications, and manage the relevant account or programme access. Do not include sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your request.

Your choices and rights

You may ask us about the personal information held about you, request correction or deletion where applicable, object to or withdraw consent for optional analytics, and raise a concern with the relevant data-protection authority. We will consider requests in accordance with applicable law.

This page is an operational privacy notice for the public GCLI site. As GCLI's activities, data locations, or service providers change, this notice will be updated before those changes take effect.
